# Logo proposals

These are the original source proposals. Proposal 01 was selected on 29 August
2026 and finished as the official browser and install icon under `public/icons`.
The transparent proposal remains here as its design source; proposals 02 and 03
remain as design history.

## 01 — Two Lights, One Garden

`01-two-lights-one-garden.png`

Two interwoven stems shelter the warm and cool lights in one living circle.
This is the most intimate proposal: it represents the two people first, with
the world growing around them.

Generation prompt:

> Create an original symbol for a private immersive world called “The Garden
> Between Us”. Two very small luminous seeds, one warm amber and one pale
> moon-blue, are held opposite each other inside a single organic circular ring
> made from two gently interweaving stems. The negative space between the stems
> suggests a protected garden clearing and togetherness without using a heart.
> Use a clean vector-like, restrained low-poly botanical style; a strong centered
> silhouette; muted moss and sage; no text, people, saturated green, watermark,
> mockup, or fragile detail.

## 02 — Five Places, One World

`02-five-places-one-world.png`

Five forms gather around one center: Tree, Wellspring, Hollow, Stars, and
Glasshouse. This is the clearest emblem of the whole world's structure.

Generation prompt:

> Create an original symbol for “The Garden Between Us”: exactly five broad,
> simple petal-like landforms arranged as one garden bloom around a central
> clearing. Their silhouettes subtly evoke a leaf/tree canopy, water droplet,
> ember flame, four-point night star, and faceted glasshouse pane. Make them one
> emblem rather than five UI icons. Use ultra-clean flat vector-like geometry,
> muted moss, sage, charcoal teal and warm ivory, with tiny amber and moon-blue
> accents; no text, number, people, watermark, mockup, or busy detail.

## 03 — The Threshold

`03-the-threshold.png`

Two trunks form an entrance; one luminous path leads into the world while the
warm and cool lights wait at its roots. This is the strongest sense of a game
world and of the invitation to “come in”.

Generation prompt:

> Create an original symbol for “The Garden Between Us”: one ancient tree trunk
> separates into two branches that curve inward as a protective doorway. Through
> it, one narrow path or river leads toward a four-point star. Two tiny lights,
> warm amber and pale moon-blue, rest at opposite roots. Use minimal vector-like
> game-emblem geometry with restrained low-poly cuts, a centered strong
> silhouette, midnight teal, dark moss, warm bark and ivory; no text, people,
> heart, wreath, watermark, mockup, saturated green, or fragile detail.

## Production result

Proposal 01 was placed on the full-bleed `#0d1512` Garden background and its
alpha-edge noise was cleaned. The master and favicon, Apple touch, 192px, and
512px exports are in `public/icons`; `index.html` and `site.webmanifest` now
reference them. The logo is not rendered anywhere inside the desktop or mobile
world itself.
